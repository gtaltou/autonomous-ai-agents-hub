# 📜 Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Autonomous AI Agents Hub community is committed to creating an open, welcoming, collaborative, and respectful environment for everyone.

We believe that innovation thrives when ideas can be shared freely, challenged constructively, and evaluated on their technical merit.

All contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to uphold this Code of Conduct.

---

# 🎯 Our Values

We promote:

- Respectful communication
- Constructive feedback
- Continuous learning
- Technical excellence
- Knowledge sharing
- Professional collaboration
- Intellectual curiosity

The goal is to create a space where engineers, researchers, students, and AI enthusiasts can learn and grow together.

---

# ✅ Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and professional
- Providing constructive feedback
- Helping others learn
- Sharing knowledge openly
- Focusing discussions on technical topics
- Accepting different viewpoints
- Being open to corrections and improvements
- Encouraging experimentation and learning

---

# 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Insults or abusive language
- Trolling or deliberate disruption
- Intimidation or threats
- Publishing private information without consent
- Spam or promotional abuse
- Repeatedly ignoring project guidelines

Disagreements are expected.

Disrespect is not.

---

# 🛠 Technical Discussions

Strong technical debate is encouraged.

When challenging ideas:

✅ Focus on the architecture

✅ Focus on the implementation

✅ Focus on the evidence

❌ Do not attack individuals

❌ Do not mock contributors

❌ Do not dismiss questions

The objective is to improve systems, not to win arguments.

---

# 📚 Learning-Friendly Environment

This repository supports engineers at different stages of their journey.

Everyone is welcome, including:

- Students
- Self-taught developers
- AI Engineers
- Software Engineers
- Data Scientists
- Researchers

Questions are encouraged.

Curiosity is a strength, not a weakness.

---

# 🤖 AI Engineering Principles

Contributors are encouraged to value:

- Reliability over hype
- Simplicity over unnecessary complexity
- Reproducibility over shortcuts
- Transparency over black-box solutions
- Evaluation over assumptions

The best AI system is not the most complex one.

The best AI system is the one that reliably solves the problem.

---

# 🔒 Security & Responsible AI

Contributors should:

- Follow security best practices
- Avoid exposing secrets or credentials
- Respect privacy and data protection principles
- Consider safety implications when designing AI systems
- Promote responsible AI usage

---

# ⚖ Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to:

- Remove inappropriate content
- Reject harmful contributions
- Restrict participation when necessary
- Enforce repository standards

Decisions will be made with the goal of maintaining a healthy and productive environment.

---

# 🌍 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- Discussions
- Documentation
- Code Reviews
- Community interactions related to this repository

---

# 🚀 Our Goal

Autonomous AI Agents Hub aims to become a high-quality reference repository for modern Agentic AI Engineering.

We build:

- Agents
- Multi-Agent Systems
- RAG Architectures
- MCP Integrations
- Production AI Systems

But most importantly, we build a community dedicated to learning, engineering excellence, and responsible innovation.
