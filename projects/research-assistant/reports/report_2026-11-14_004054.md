## Fact Check Notes

The claims are broadly supported by mainstream historical scholarship, but no external sources were supplied or independently cited. The phrase “river-valley civilizations” may oversimplify the origins of complex societies, since important developments also occurred beyond major river valleys. Greece and Rome are emphasized in a way that may underrepresent Persia, South Asia, Africa, East Asia, and the Americas. The medieval period was not a single uniform era worldwide, and the Industrial Revolution began in Britain before spreading unevenly. The confidence score of 0.98 is plausible for the broad claims but too high if interpreted as confidence in completeness or detailed precision.

---

## Final Conclusion

The summary is factually sound as a concise introductory framework, but it should be presented as selective rather than comprehensive. Greater regional balance, chronological specificity, and cited evidence would improve its accuracy and usefulness.
