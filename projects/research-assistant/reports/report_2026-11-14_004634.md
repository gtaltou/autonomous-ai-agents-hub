## Topic

History as the Evidence-Based Study of the Past

---

## Executive Summary

History is the systematic study and interpretation of human societies, events, and change over time. Historians use primary evidence and secondary scholarship, while accounting for gaps, bias, context, and differing methods. Historical inquiry helps explain the origins and development of contemporary institutions, cultures, conflicts, and identities.

---

## Key Findings

- History investigates human events, societies, and transformations across time.
- Primary sources provide direct or near-contemporary evidence; secondary works analyze and interpret that evidence.
- Historical interpretations can differ because evidence is incomplete and sources, perspectives, contexts, and methodologies vary.
- Historical knowledge offers context for understanding modern institutions, cultures, conflicts, and social identities.
